```css
🟡 Neon Yellow:      #FFEE32  (Background - LOUD & ATTENTION-GRABBING)

🟠 Vibrant Orange:   #FF6B35  (Secondary - Heat & Action)
```

✅ **Hard Shadows** - No soft, corporate vibes
✅ **High Contrast** - Maximum readability, accessibility
✅ **Chaotic Energy** - Reflects Gen Z's rejection of minimalism

## 🎯 Gen Z Design Principles Applied

### 1. **THICK BORDERS EVERYWHERE**
```jsx
border-[4px] border-[#0A0A0A]  // Cards
border-[3px] border-[#0A0A0A]  // Stickers
```
- No thin, delicate lines
- Bold, chunky, impossible to miss
- Creates strong visual hierarchy

### 2. **HARD DROP SHADOWS**
```jsx
shadow-[8px_8px_0px_#0A0A0A]  // Large cards
shadow-[6px_6px_0px_#0A0A0A]  // Medium elements
shadow-[4px_4px_0px_#0A0A0A]  // Small badges
```
- Offset shadows (not centered)
- Solid colors (not gradients)
- Creates depth without blur

### 3. **CHAOTIC ROTATION**
```jsx
-rotate-6, rotate-3, -rotate-12, rotate-2
```
- Nothing is perfectly aligned
- Stickers scattered randomly
- Cards tilted at angles
- Feels handmade, authentic

### 4. **ALL CAPS TYPOGRAPHY**
```jsx
CRAFTING THE WEB LOUDER
NOT YOUR AVERAGE DEV
SHIP FAST, BREAK THINGS
```
- Screaming energy
- No lowercase politeness
- Bold, confident, loud

### 5. **LAYERED TEXT SHADOWS**
```jsx
textShadow: "6px 6px 0px #00D9FF, 12px 12px 0px #FF6B35"
```
- Double shadows in different colors
- Creates 3D depth
- Retro gaming aesthetic

### 6. **INLINE HIGHLIGHTED TEXT**
```jsx
<span className="bg-[#00D9FF] px-2 py-1 border-[2px] border-[#0A0A0A]">
  BOLD
</span>
```
- Words get their own boxes
- Like highlighting with a marker
- Emphasizes key concepts
